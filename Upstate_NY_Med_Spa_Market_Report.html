Clinics vs. search demand

Reading clinic supply against consumer search interest side by side.

  • Botox's supply lead tracks its (New York DMA) search dominance closely. 22 of 27 clinics (81%) offer it, and it's also the runaway search leader (index 81, by far the highest of any term compared). Dermal / Lip Fillers is a close second in supply (21 clinics, 78%) but searches far behind (index 13) — the same supply-ahead-of-demand gap seen in almost every metro in this project.
  • Medical Weight Loss / GLP-1 is again the widest demand-ahead-of-supply mismatch. Only 2 of 27 clinics (7%) offer it, yet "Semaglutide" posts a search index of 34 in the New York DMA — second only to Botox.
  • Laser Hair Removal and Body Contouring / CoolSculpting are the only mid-tier supply categories (7 and 5 clinics respectively), both well below their New York DMA search indices (33 and 3) — LHR in particular looks under-supplied relative to demand in this dataset.

Every other metro researched so far showed zero confirmed Sculptra supply despite nonzero search interest (index 7); this is the first data point closing that gap, even if only partially.

Are we comparing the same thing?

Short answer: rarely — pricing transparency here is nearly as low as the NJ Gold Coast corridor, the lowest two metros in this project.

  • Botox's clean per-unit pricing comes from only 2 of 22 clinics — The Formula MedSpa, Rye ($11/unit, a current limited-time special) and Youtherapy Medspa's Scarsdale and Chappaqua locations ($12/unit, but only for $99/month Platinum members — the non-member rate isn't published). Medical Aesthetics of the Hudson Valley publishes a much wider $280–$1,200 figure that reads as a per-session or per-area price rather than a per-unit rate, and Hebe Medical Spa's membership tiers discount an unpublished base rate by $1–$2/unit — neither is included in the clean per-unit range above. The other 19 clinics offering Botox in this dataset do not publish pricing.
  • Dermal / Lip Fillers ($500–$850/syringe) comes from a single source — Medical Aesthetics of the Hudson Valley (1 of 21 clinics). The Formula MedSpa's $659 "Bio-Filler Facelift" package and buy-2-get-1-free syringe promo, and Hebe Medical Spa's $100-off-per-syringe membership perk, are excluded as non-comparable package/discount pricing rather than a clean per-syringe rate.
  • Every other category in this dataset is "Not widely published." Park Avenue Medical Spa, Armonk markets itself as "the premier CoolSculpting provider in Westchester County" without listing a price; The Formula MedSpa's "30% off Ultherapy" is a percentage discount off an unpublished base rate, not a price.
  • What we did about it: every "# clinics" figure in this report counts distinct clinics, not raw price listings, and every procedure name is clickable so you can see exactly which clinic and listing a number came from.
  • 19% of clinics in this dataset show any pricing at all, full or partial — the second-lowest figure in this project, just above Hudson & Bergen Co., NJ's 16% and well below every other metro (31–77%). Two consult-first, high-net-worth corridors in a row posting the lowest transparency scores in this project suggests affluent, low-volume, membership-driven markets are simply less likely to publish rate cards than higher-volume urban/suburban markets — not a research-methodology artifact specific to either metro.
