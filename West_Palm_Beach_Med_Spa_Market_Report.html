Clinics vs. search demand

Reading clinic supply against consumer search interest side by side.

  • Botox leads on both supply and demand, the same pattern seen in Miami. Botox / Neuromodulators is both the most-offered category by distinct clinic count (45 of 59 clinics, 76%) and the runaway search leader (index 74). Dermal / Lip Fillers is the second most-offered category (39 clinics, 66%) but searches at essentially 0 — West Palm Beach searchers likely split filler searches across brand names (Juvéderm, Restylane, Sculptra) rather than the generic term, the same understatement pattern seen in Atlanta and Miami.
  • Microneedling and IPL / Photofacial are the best-aligned categories on the demand side. Both post the highest non-Botox search indexes (35 and 34) even though their clinic supply is comparatively modest (20 clinics/34% and 16 clinics/27%, the 4th and 7th most-offered categories) — real consumer interest that clinic supply hasn't fully caught up to yet.
  • Medical Weight Loss / GLP-1 is the starkest demand-ahead-of-supply mismatch in this dataset. Only 11 of 59 clinics offer it (19%, tied for 9th most-offered) yet it posts a search index of 33 — the third-highest demand in the whole set, reflecting the broader Semaglutide/GLP-1 wave outpacing local clinic listings.
  • Body Contouring / CoolSculpting is the starkest supply-ahead-of-demand mismatch. It's the 3rd most-offered category by clinic count (25 of 59 clinics, 42%) yet its search index is just 6 — among the lowest in the whole set, and it also carries almost no published pricing (see below). Searchers may be using brand or device names ("CoolSculpting" itself scores low here too) rather than the generic category term.
  • Kybella and Skin Tightening (Morpheus8/RF) are thin on both sides. Kybella sits at just 1 of 59 clinics (2%) and a search index of 1; Skin Tightening has more supply (12 clinics, 20%) but an equally low search index of 1 — both remain niche, low-visibility categories in this market.

Are we comparing the same thing?

Short answer: not always — and that's worth knowing before reading too much into any single price range above.

  • Botox's published per-unit pricing comes from just 4 of 45 clinics. AlluraMD Wellington ($8.95/unit VIP, $12/unit regular), Alluring Image Medspa Delray Beach (from $14/unit), and Facial Mania Delray Beach (from $8.99/unit) are the only genuinely comparable per-unit rates found. Unlike Atlanta and Miami, no repeated boilerplate pricing phrase turned up across unrelated West Palm Beach clinics — where pricing exists here, it reads as real.
  • Dermal / Lip Fillers ($499–$840/syringe) is a thin sample from just 2 clinics out of 39 offering fillers — the other 37 publish no filler pricing at all.
  • Body Contouring / CoolSculpting (25 clinics) has essentially no genuine pricing — the one price found (Rejuvenation Med Spa's vaginal-rejuvenation package) isn't really a body-contouring rate, so this category should be read as "supply without any visible pricing signal."
  • IPL / Photofacial has a category-overlap flag: Rejuvenation Med Spa's "Scarlet RF Microneedling" package matched the IPL/Photofacial keyword list due to the shared "RF" term, but it's really a microneedling treatment — excluded from that category's price range for this reason.
  • What we did about it: every "# clinics" figure in this report counts distinct clinics, not raw price listings. We can't fully normalize prices to a common unit automatically, since clinics describe quantities and package sizes in inconsistent free text, so every procedure name is clickable and opens the full list of underlying listings — exact wording, exact price, per clinic — so you can check whether two prices are actually for the same thing before comparing them.
  • Only 31% of West Palm Beach clinics show any pricing at all, full or partial — similar to Atlanta (32%) and well below Charlotte (70%). Most West Palm Beach med spas lean on "book a free consultation" rather than a published rate card.
