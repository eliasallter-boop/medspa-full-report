Coverage at a glance

Since a single citywide Google Maps search consistently capped at 6 visible results, coverage was built from 15 neighborhood/suburb-scoped searches instead across the Houston metro (Downtown, Midtown, Montrose, River Oaks, Heights, West University, Memorial, Galleria, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Cypress, Pearland, Clear Lake, and Spring), turning up 108 distinct candidates (101 logged as trackable). 4 were dropped as non-medical spas during research (IV-hydration-only businesses and a non-medical beauty studio), 3 were unverifiable (no matching business found), and 1 bonus second location of an existing chain was discovered along the way — leaving 95 confirmed medical spas.

Search interest in Houston (Google Trends)

Relative search interest for each treatment among Houston–area searchers over the past 12 months, indexed 0–100 against the single highest-volume term (Botox) across all comparisons. One representative search term was used per category — see note below.

Source: trends.google.com, geo = Houston, TX (Nielsen DMA US-TX-618), Aug 2025–Aug 2026, weekly values averaged and read directly from the Trends API response rather than estimated visually, using a single Chrome tab with spaced-out requests (no parallel tabs). Google Trends indexes relative search volume for the exact term entered, not category-wide demand — e.g. "Dermal Fillers" doesn't capture searches for "Juvéderm" or "Restylane," and "IPL Photofacial" doesn't capture "BBL" or "Lumecca." Treat this as a directional signal, not an absolute demand figure.

Clinics vs. search demand

Reading clinic supply against consumer search interest side by side.

  • Botox leads both supply and demand. Botox / Neuromodulators is the most-offered category (62 of 95 clinics, 65%) and posts the highest search index of any city researched so far (82). Dermal / Lip Fillers is the second most-offered category (53 clinics, 56%) but searches at just index 1.
  • Laser Hair Removal shows the strongest demand-outpacing-supply signal of any mid-tier category in this project. It's only 6th most-offered (26 clinics, 27%) yet posts the highest Laser Hair Removal search index of any city (41), well ahead of its supply rank. Microneedling is better matched on both sides (3rd supply at 31 clinics/33%, 2nd demand at 34).
  • Medical Weight Loss / GLP-1 is again the starkest raw demand-ahead-of-supply mismatch. Just 12 of 95 clinics offer it (13%, tied 10th) yet it posts a search index of 59, the third-highest GLP-1 figure across all cities researched.
  • Body Contouring / CoolSculpting is the clearest supply-ahead-of-demand mismatch. It's the 4th most-offered category (29 clinics, 31%) yet its search index is just 3.
  • Kybella and PRP / PRF are thin or invisible on the demand side. Kybella sits at 6 of 95 clinics (6%) with an index of 1; PRP / PRF has real supply (11 clinics, 12%) but essentially zero search interest (index 0).

Are we comparing the same thing?

Short answer: not always — and that's worth knowing before reading too much into any single price range above.

  • Botox's published per-unit pricing comes from just 4 of 62 clinics, and two of those four (River Oaks Galleria Medspa and River Oaks MedSpa) may be the same ownership group publishing identical rates under two names. 6 unrelated clinics (Tulum Wellness Spa, TLC MedSpa Houston, Amora Med Spa, Persona Medical Spa, PurA Medical Aesthetics, SkinSpirit Houston - Rice Village) show the exact same '$12-$15/unit' phrasing seen repeated verbatim in every prior city of this project — treated as generic boilerplate estimate text and excluded from this report's range. A second aggregator pattern ('$200-$900/session,' matching Dallas's Elite Med Spa of Texas figures) appeared at 2 more clinics and was also excluded.
  • Dermal / Lip Fillers ($550–$750/syringe) comes from just 3 of 53 clinics, two of which may share ownership. The same boilerplate ('$600-$1,200/syringe,' 7 clinics) and aggregator ('$600-$2,000/syringe,' 2 clinics) contamination patterns appear here too and were excluded.
  • Microneedling (31 clinics) mixes a $525 standard SkinPen promo with an $800-$2,288 RF Microneedling package — two different technologies at very different price points, not a single comparable rate.
  • Skin Tightening ($488–$2,588) is a single clinic's Ultherapy price list across five treatment areas — not a market range at all; treat this figure with caution.
  • What we did about it: every "# clinics" figure in this report counts distinct clinics, not raw price listings. We can't fully normalize prices to a common unit automatically, since clinics describe quantities and package sizes in inconsistent free text, so every procedure name is clickable and opens the full list of underlying listings — exact wording, exact price, per clinic — so you can check whether two prices are actually for the same thing before comparing them.
  • 56% of Houston clinics show any pricing at all, full or partial — in the middle of the range seen in this project so far, below Charlotte (70%) and Orlando (65%), but above Atlanta and Miami (32%) and West Palm Beach (31%). A cluster of independent clinics in Katy and Cypress running visible promo and Groupon pricing drives most of this.
