Coverage at a glance

Since a single citywide Google Maps search consistently capped at 6 visible results, coverage was built from 15 neighborhood/suburb-scoped searches instead (Midtown, Downtown, Old Fourth Ward, Virginia-Highland, Buckhead, Brookhaven, Sandy Springs, Dunwoody, Decatur, Vinings, Chamblee, Grant Park/Summerhill, and more), turning up 60 distinct candidates. 13 were dropped after closer review: 7 were clearly non-medspa businesses (massage-only, head-spa, or day-spa chains), 1 was a confirmed-closed clinic (replaced at the same address by its successor), 2 could not be verified as real businesses at their listed address, and 2 turned out to be facials/waxing-only studios despite ambiguous names — leaving 47 confirmed medical spas.

Google Trends indexes relative search volume for the exact term entered, not category-wide demand — e.g. "Microneedling" doesn't capture searches for "Morpheus8," and "PRP Injection" doesn't capture "PRP facial." Clinics vs. search demand

Reading clinic supply against consumer search interest side by side.

  • Fillers out-supply Botox, but Botox dominates search by a wide margin. Dermal/lip fillers are the single most-offered category by distinct clinic count (36 of 47 clinics), narrowly ahead of Botox (34 clinics) — yet Botox is the runaway leader on search, with an index of 83 versus the generic term "Lip Filler" at just 16. Atlanta searchers likely split filler searches across brand names (Juvéderm, Restylane, Sculptra) rather than the generic term, so true filler-category demand is probably understated here.
  • Microneedling and Laser Hair Removal are the best-aligned categories. Both post solid mid-tier search indexes (37 and 34) alongside strong clinic counts (31 and 21), suggesting healthy alignment between what's searched and what's offered.
  • Chemical Peels are a supply-heavy outlier. 23 of 47 clinics offer peels — the third-most-common category — yet the search index sits at just 12, well behind Microneedling and Laser Hair Removal despite comparable clinic supply. Peels may be sold more as an add-on to a facial than something clients actively search for by name.
  • Kybella, CoolSculpting, Emsculpt, Ultherapy, and PDO Thread Lift all show low single-digit search indexes (0–6) despite real, if modest, clinic supply (2–7 clinics each). That gap suggests either different search phrasing is in play locally (e.g. "double chin treatment," "fat freezing near me") or clinic supply in these categories is running ahead of measurable local search demand.
  • PDO Thread Lift and Ultherapy are the rarest categories in the Atlanta dataset — only 2 and 3 of 47 clinics offer them, respectively. Both are premium device/technique investments, and in this dataset they appear concentrated at Ecobel Med Spa and a small handful of others rather than being widely adopted across the market.

Are we comparing the same thing?

Short answer: not always — and that's worth knowing before reading too much into any single price range above.

  • Botox's published per-unit pricing is thin, and possibly not all clinic-specific. Only Ecobel Med Spa publishes a firm rate ($12–$15/unit across Botox/Dysport/Xeomin). The identical figure — "$12–$15 per unit" — also turned up for three unrelated clinics (Restore Wellness and MedSpa, Pure Bliss Medspa, and beauty Bar Med Spa) with matching wording, which reads more like a generic market-typical estimate surfaced by search than each clinic's own posted price. Those three are marked "Partial" pricing rather than "Yes" for that reason — treat their numbers as directional only.
  • Dermal/Lip Fillers ($499–$3,400) mixes single-syringe rates with full combination treatments. The $499 low end is dermani MEDSPA's standard Juvéderm syringe price (consistent across its 5 Atlanta-area locations). The $3,400 high end is Ecobel Med Spa's "Liquid Face Lift," a multi-area combination filler treatment — not a single syringe. Click "Dermal / Lip Fillers" above to see the exact wording for every listing.
  • Laser Hair Removal ($50–$400) blends per-session and monthly-membership pricing. Ecobel prices by the session ($100–$200); dermani's 5 Atlanta-area locations instead sell laser hair removal as a monthly membership ($50–$200/month). A session price and a subscription price aren't directly comparable, even though both are labeled "Laser Hair Removal."
  • PRP Therapy ($475–$5,000) spans an add-on and a full package. $475 is dermani Dunwoody's SkinPen-plus-PRP microneedling add-on; $5,000 is Ecobel's top-tier exosome/PRP hair-restoration package (2 treatments). Same category label, very different treatments and commitment levels.
  • What we did about it: every "# clinics" figure in this report counts distinct clinics, not raw price listings (a clinic offering both Botox and Dysport only counts once under "Botox / Neuromodulators"). We can't fully normalize prices to a common unit automatically, since clinics describe quantities and package sizes in inconsistent free text, so instead every procedure name is clickable and opens the full list of underlying listings — exact wording, exact price, per clinic — so you can check whether two prices are actually for the same thing before comparing them.
  • Not every listing has a price at all. Most Atlanta clinics require a consultation before quoting, especially for injectables — so most category price ranges here are built from a small minority of listings (for example, only 1 of 14 tracked PRP listings outside Ecobel had a firm price). A real range, just a thin one.
